# Vendor Registry

`backend/e4h-services/vendor-registry`

## What it does

A generic registry (also referred to in its own README as the "Organisation Service") storing every type of organisation — vendors, contractors, community-based organisations — including contact details, tax identifiers, and areas of work. In Livelihood it owns Vendor Organisations and Platform Organisations, org users, and jurisdiction/state assignment. Depends on the DIGIT backbone, Persister, Indexer, IDGen, and the Individual service.
